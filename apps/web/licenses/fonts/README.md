These are the SIL Open Font License notices for the unmodified Geist, Geist Mono,
and Geist Pixel WOFF2 subsets served from `src/lib/assets/fonts/`.

The subsets match the Google Fonts stylesheet previously requested by the site,
including its language coverage, normal weights, and Geist italic style. They
were downloaded from `fonts.gstatic.com` on 2026-10-29.

The license notices come from Google's font repository:

- [Geist](https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/geist/OFL.txt)
- [Geist Mono](https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/geistmono/OFL.txt)
